Medium Chungus Recipe

Description

This is a recipe for cooking medium chungus. By the end of this recipe, you should have 1 perfectly cooked and tender medium chungus. This serves 3 people.

Medium chungus is a great meal for people of all types.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium chungus
  • 1 oven
  • Some salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Pluck the medium chungus
  2. Salt and pepper the medium chungus
  3. Bake medium chungus for 2 hours at 350 degrees F
  4. Enjoy!
